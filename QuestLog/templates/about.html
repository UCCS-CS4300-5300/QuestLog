About QuestLog

QuestLog turns everyday chores and responsibilities into a game-like quest system for groups, roommates, and teams.

Task Management

Create tasks, assign them to a party, and keep track of what still needs to be done.

Difficulty & Points

Each task has a difficulty from 1 to 10, and points scale with that difficulty to reward harder work.

Recurring Tasks

Repeating chores can be set up as recurring tasks so important responsibilities stay visible over time.

Parties & Collaboration

Users can create parties, invite members, and work together inside a shared task pool.

How QuestLog Works

  • Users join or create a party.
  • Tasks are added to that party’s shared quest pool.
  • Difficulty is rated on a 1 to 10 scale.
  • Points are based on difficulty × 10.
  • Members can complete tasks and submit proof when needed.
  • Recurring tasks can be reused for repeated responsibilities.

Stakeholders

  • Primary stakeholders: customers and end users
  • Secondary stakeholders: the development team
